# Legal Data Sources & Strategy

## Authoritative Hierarchy

1. **Tier 1 (Authority 5): Official Primary Sources**
   - **India Code** (`https://www.indiacode.nic.in/`): Central Acts, State Acts, Rules, Regulations, Notifications, Ordinances.
   - **Legislative Department** (`https://www.legislative.gov.in/`): Constitution of India, Constitution Amendment Acts.
   - **Supreme Court of India** (`https://www.sci.gov.in/`): Supreme Court Judgments and Orders.
   - **eCourts Portal** (`https://judgments.ecourts.gov.in/`): High Court & District Court Judgments.

2. **Tier 2 (Authority 2-3): Supplementary Sources**
   - **Law Commission of India** (`https://lawcommissionofindia.nic.in/`): Law Commission Reports & Reform Recommendations (Authority 3).
   - **Indian Kanoon** (`https://indiankanoon.org/`): Supplementary legal database (Authority 2). Cites primary sources where available.
